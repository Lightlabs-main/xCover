Opening a position immediately mints a non-transferable xCover Policy NFT (XCOVER). The NFT records the owner, amount, price, term, commitments, and lifecycle; the waiting period gates claim evaluation, not withdrawal.

CLAIM FLOW

The existing policy NFT becomes eligible for evaluation after its waiting period. A claim requires a covered trigger and the required observation history.

Evaluation and payout are permissionless. The payout goes to the XCOVER NFT owner; it is not a replacement NFT.
